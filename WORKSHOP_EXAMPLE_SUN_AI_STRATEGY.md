# SUN AI Strategy Workshop (30 minutes)
## Justice-Centered AI Maturity Planning

A complete AI strategy session using the four-lens framework (Work, Risk, Sustainability, Ethics) to identify opportunities, assess risks, and build guardrails.

---

## Workshop Structure

### **Phase 1: Discovery & Opportunity (10 minutes)**
*Map where AI could strengthen SUN's work and surface key risks*

#### **Breakout Round 1: AI Opportunity Scan (5 min)**

**Question 1.1: Where could AI strengthen SUN's work?**
- **Response Type:** `written` (text + voting)
- **Lens:** Work
- **Enable Voting:** ✅ Yes
- **Purpose:** Generate ideas, vote to identify top 5-7 opportunities

**Question 1.2: Map opportunities by impact and readiness**
- **Response Type:** `landscape` (2D positioning)
- **Lens:** Work
- **X-Axis Label:** "Strategic Impact"
  - Min: "Low impact" (0)
  - Max: "Transforms our mission" (10)
  - Prompt: "How much could this amplify our impact?"
- **Y-Axis Label:** "Organizational Readiness"
  - Min: "Not ready" (0)
  - Max: "Ready to pilot" (10)
  - Prompt: "How prepared are we to implement this responsibly?"
- **Purpose:** Quick wins = top-right quadrant; long-term bets = top-left

**→ Dashboards Available:** Overview, Roadmap, Response Landscape, Timeline, Chat

---

#### **Breakout Round 2: Risk Identification (5 min)**

**Question 1.3: What risks concern you most?**
- **Response Type:** `multiSelect` (pick multiple)
- **Lens:** Risk
- **Options:**
  - Work: Staff displacement, skill gaps, over-reliance on automation
  - Community: Privacy violations, algorithmic bias, digital divide
  - Sustainability: Energy consumption of AI systems, e-waste
  - Ethics: Vendor lock-in, mission drift, greenwashing
  - Governance: Lack of oversight, unclear accountability
  - Equity: Excluding marginalized voices, reinforcing inequities
- **Purpose:** Surface collective concerns across all lenses

**Question 1.4: How confident are we in managing each risk area?**
- **Response Type:** `scale` (0-10 slider)
- **Lens:** Risk
- **Scale Labels:**
  - Min: "Not confident" (0)
  - Max: "Very confident" (10)
- **Purpose:** Gap analysis - high concern + low confidence = urgent action

**→ Dashboards Available:** Overview, Heatmap (risk × confidence), Timeline, Chat

---

### **Phase 2: Guardrails & Principles (10 minutes)**
*Define non-negotiables for responsible AI implementation*

#### **Breakout Round 1: Sustainability Commitments (5 min)**

**Question 2.1: Which sustainability commitments should guide our AI choices?**
- **Response Type:** `multiSelect`
- **Lens:** Sustainability
- **Options:**
  - Prioritize energy-efficient AI models
  - Require renewable energy for AI infrastructure
  - Minimize data storage to reduce carbon footprint
  - Choose vendors with strong climate commitments
  - Track and report AI-related emissions
  - Favor open-source over proprietary systems
- **Purpose:** Build sustainability criteria for AI vendor/tool decisions

**→ Dashboards Available:** Overview, Heatmap (commitment priorities), Chat

---

#### **Breakout Round 2: Ethical Guardrails (5 min)**

**Question 2.2: What ethical principles are non-negotiable?**
- **Response Type:** `written` (text + voting)
- **Lens:** Ethics
- **Enable Voting:** ✅ Yes
- **Purpose:** Co-create ethical guardrails; voting reveals consensus principles

**Question 2.3: Rate current AI maturity**
- **Response Type:** `scale` (0-10 slider)
- **Lens:** Ethics
- **Scale Labels:**
  - Min: "Just starting" (0)
  - Max: "Fully mature" (10)
- **Purpose:** Baseline maturity assessment for tracking progress

**→ Dashboards Available:** Overview, Heatmap, Roadmap, Timeline, Chat

---

### **Phase 3: Roadmap & Commitments (10 minutes)**
*Convert insights into actionable strategy with ownership*

#### **Breakout Round 1: Action Sequencing (5 min)**

**Question 3.1: When should we tackle each priority?**
- **Response Type:** `written` (text + voting)
- **Lens:** Work
- **Enable Voting:** ✅ Yes
- **Purpose:** Place top opportunities on Now/Next/Later timeline
  - High votes (6+) → "Now" (immediate action)
  - Medium votes (3-5) → "Next" (Q2-Q3)
  - Low votes (1-2) → "Later" (future consideration)
  - No votes → "Signal" (monitor trends)

**→ Dashboards Available:** Overview, Roadmap (priority swimlanes), Timeline, Leaderboard, Chat

---

#### **Breakout Round 2: Personal Commitments (5 min)**

**Question 3.2: What will you personally commit to advancing our AI maturity?**
- **Response Type:** `written` (text + voting)
- **Lens:** Agency
- **Enable Voting:** ✅ Yes
- **Purpose:** Individual accountability; voting identifies champions and cross-functional allies

**Question 3.3: Position your commitment on timeline**
- **Response Type:** `landscape` (2D positioning)
- **Lens:** Agency
- **X-Axis Label:** "Time to Impact"
  - Min: "This month" (0)
  - Max: "6+ months" (10)
- **Y-Axis Label:** "Effort Required"
  - Min: "Low effort" (0)
  - Max: "Significant effort" (10)
- **Purpose:** Reality check - identify quick wins vs long-term initiatives

**→ Dashboards Available:** Overview, Roadmap, Response Landscape, Leaderboard, Chat

---

## Session Outputs

By the end of 30 minutes, you'll have:

### 📊 **AI Value Map**
- Response Landscape showing quick wins (high impact, high readiness) vs long-term investments
- Voted list of top 5-7 opportunities

### 🛡️ **Risk Profile**
- Heatmap revealing concern clusters across Work/Community/Sustainability/Ethics/Governance/Equity
- Confidence gap analysis (high concern + low confidence = urgent capacity building)

### 🌱 **Sustainability Framework**
- Voted commitments for energy, carbon, vendor selection, and open-source priorities
- Criteria checklist for evaluating AI tools and vendors

### ⚖️ **Ethical Guardrails**
- Consensus principles (voted by team)
- Baseline maturity score to track progress over time

### 🗺️ **Action Roadmap**
- Now/Next/Later prioritization driven by voting
- Commitment landscape showing quick wins vs ambitious long-term goals
- Champion network (voted commitments identify who's leading what)

## Why This Works

✅ **Four-Lens Integration** - Each phase addresses Work, Risk, Sustainability, or Ethics explicitly

✅ **Response Type Diversity** - Uses all available input methods:
- `written` → qualitative insights with voting
- `landscape` → 2D strategic positioning
- `multiSelect` → categorical risk/commitment selection
- `scale` → quantitative maturity/confidence ratings

✅ **Progressive Build** - Each phase builds on previous insights:
1. Discovery → identifies opportunities and risks
2. Guardrails → establishes principles and commitments
3. Roadmap → converts into action with ownership

✅ **Justice-Centered Design**:
- Equity risks explicitly named in multi-select options
- Multi-select ensures minority voices heard (not just majority)
- Voting amplifies collective wisdom while preserving dissent
- Landscape maps prevent groupthink (individual positioning)

✅ **Auto-Configured Dashboards** - Frontend intelligently shows only compatible visualizations based on question response types

✅ **Action-Oriented** - Ends with concrete roadmap, personal commitments, and identified champions

## Facilitator Tips

**Phase 1 (Discovery):**
- Encourage wild ideas in Q1.1 - no judgment
- In landscape mapping (Q1.2), emphasize honest self-assessment
- Use risk multi-select (Q1.3) to normalize naming hard truths

**Phase 2 (Guardrails):**
- Frame sustainability (Q2.1) as competitive advantage, not constraint
- Let ethical principles (Q2.2) emerge organically - don't pre-script
- Maturity rating (Q2.3) is baseline - celebrate wherever you are

**Phase 3 (Roadmap):**
- Tie actions (Q3.1) directly to Phase 1 landscape quick wins
- Personal commitments (Q3.2) should be specific and achievable
- Commitment landscape (Q3.3) helps people right-size their promises

**Dashboard Usage:**
- Start each phase with Overview to ground everyone
- Switch to Heatmap when clustering/patterns emerge
- Use Roadmap to show prioritization in real-time
- End with Leaderboard to celebrate participation

This creates a complete, justice-centered AI strategy in 30 minutes with actionable visual artifacts you can reference for the next year! 🎯
